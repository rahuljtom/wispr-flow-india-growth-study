# Analysis notes (P6, modules not dependent on review tagging)

## A4 scenario decomposition (identity, not causal)
Revenue-per-install index = 2% / 14% = 0.143 (R-011). Price index = 3.4 / 12 = 0.283 (R-012; India price as reported with TechCrunch's own USD conversion). Residual = 0.504 (R-013), which lumps conversion, mix, measurement and anything else. Sensitivity grid (missed India revenue 0-60% x India price applying to 50%-100% of the window; price start assumed 2025-12-01, UNVERIFIED day) gives residual 0.22-1.22 and revenue-per-install 0.14-0.35 (R-014, R-015). The residual range spans values from well below to above 1, so the gap cannot be apportioned between price and everything else from public data. Table: `data/processed/decomposition_scenarios.csv`.

## A7 experiment sizing
statsmodels `NormalIndPower` on Cohen's h, alpha 0.05 two-sided, power 0.8, equal arms; duration = 2n / (1,650 x android_share x eligible x exposed) over android 30/50/70% (E3 platform-agnostic: 100%), eligible 50/80%, exposed 50/100%. Table: `data/processed/experiment_sizing.csv` (252 rows). **Note:** an earlier planning expectation "baseline 0.30 vs 0.33 is about 1,800 per arm" does not match the standard formula; both the arcsine method and the pooled two-proportion formula give about 3,770 per arm. The code and tests use the standard result; the earlier figure is not used. E2 was redefined on 2026-10-18 as an Android free-tier cap-level test, because current official docs cap the Android free tier (the launch-era unlimited offer is not documented as current).

## A3 Karnataka signal (Google Trends, "wispr flow", provisional campaign date 2026-04-27)
**Not estimable** as a difference-in-differences. Usable geos (positive pre-period mean, at least 10 non-zero pre-period weeks of 35, at most 10% missing): IN-KA and IN-MH only. IN-DL, IN-TN and IN-TG have 4-7 non-zero pre-period weeks of 35 and are too sparse; at least two usable controls are required. The three misspelling series have no usable pre-period in any geo. Descriptively, interest rose after the date in every geo including the controls (Maharashtra pre-mean 25 to post-mean 62; Karnataka 26 to 73 on the raw 0-100 scale), so a Karnataka-specific change cannot be separated from a national one, and the same period includes the Hinglish launch and other activity. The chart (`outputs/charts/slide8_trends_event_study.png`) is descriptive and shows only the two usable geos. Slide 8 therefore uses the "not estimable" variant with a proposed measurement design.

## Audit notes (2026-10-18)
- **Decomposition comparability.** Revenue-per-install index = India revenue share / India install share, which is relative to the all-markets rate (India included). The price index compares annual-plan list prices (3.4 vs 12 USD); the 3.4 comes from TechCrunch's own conversion of Rs 320, so FX is implied and rounded: a true USD figure of 3.35 to 3.45 moves the index only between 0.279 and 0.288, small against the grid range. Not captured: realised prices, discounts and promotions, the free tier, monthly-plan mix, student plans and billing route (web billing via Stripe is outside an in-app-purchase measure; docs R-085).
- **Observation windows.** Installs and revenue shares cover Oct 2025 to Apr 2026 (212 days). India pricing began in December (year not stated by TechCrunch; day assumed 1 Dec); Android launched 2026-02-23; the India launch and Bengaluru campaign fall in the last days of the window (2026-04-27). The grid covers price applying to 50%, 71% and 100% of the window; it does not model changing install mix inside the window.
- **Experiments.** Baselines and MDEs are illustrative assumptions, not estimates. Duration = enrolment (2n over traffic) plus a follow-up window (E1 7 days for the D7 uninstall guardrail, E2 14 days for day-14 conversion, E3 7 days assumed), reported as `total_days`. Guardrails are not separately powered. E3 counts invited users but traffic is installs per day, so its duration is a rough proxy. About 1,650 installs per day is a whole-window average and probably understates current traffic after the India launch (unknown). Kill criteria are decision rules whose numeric limits need internal baselines.
- **Reviews.** Tagged subset = newest 321 of the 600 selected Play reviews (2026-07-30 to 2026-10-17, no App Store); the roman-Hindi/Devanagari proxy has no reviews in it (5 in the full selection). 'Other' is the largest theme (31%) and is excluded from the headline list on slide 4. Low-rated (one or two stars) n is 52.
